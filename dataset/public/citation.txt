# Dataset Citations & Public Data Inventory

## Primary Study (2-2-1 Blended Learning & Power/Fuel Crisis Survey)
- **Title**: Perceptions of College Students Toward Blended Learning as a Response to Power Fuel Crisis
- **Authors**: Johann Jethro O. Chavez, Mikylla Nicole M. Nañez, Divine Mae S. Ponciano
- **Institution**: Camarines Norte State College (CNSC), Daet, Camarines Norte, Philippines
- **Sample Size**: 123 raw / 117 cleaned student respondents (Combined Multi-Source Total: 1,530 raw / 1,427 cleaned records)
- **File**: `dataset/Perceptions of College Students Toward Blended Learning as a Response to Power Fuel Crisis.csv`

---

## Public Study 1 (Blended Learning Perceptions, Engagement & Academic Achievement)
- **Title**: Data on student perceptions of blended learning, engagement, and academic achievement in engineering-related universities in Vietnam
- **Authors**: Hoai Thu Le, Hieu Hoc Le, Thanh Hai Thi Pham
- **Year**: 2026
- **Journal**: *Data in Brief*, Volume 67, 112958
- **Mendeley Data DOI**: [10.17632/tdsspksw83.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/tdsspksw83.1)
- **Sample Size**: 580 raw / 508 validated undergraduate responses across eight multidisciplinary and engineering-oriented universities (72 straight-line/careless responses screened out)
- **File**: `dataset/public/Le_2026_VN_Blended_Learning_Perceptions/Blended learning_data set.xlsx`
- **Key Constructs Mapped**: Overall Learning Experience (`LE1`), Cognitive Presence / Lesson Comprehension (`CP4`), Instructional Design / Schedule Fit (`IDQ2`, `IDQ5`), Academic Achievement (`AA2`), and Student Engagement / Retention (`SE2`).

---

## Public Study 2 (Undergraduate Self-Regulation in Online and Blended Learning Environments)
- **Title**: Data on undergraduate students' self-regulation in online and blended learning environments during the COVID-19 pandemic in Indonesia
- **Author**: Irwanto Irwanto
- **Year**: 2024
- **Institution**: State University of Jakarta, Indonesia
- **Journal**: *Data in Brief*, Volume 53, 110066
- **Mendeley Data DOI**: [10.17632/7bsvck73ng.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/7bsvck73ng.1)
- **Sample Size**: 577 raw / 573 validated undergraduate student respondents (4 straight-line/careless responses screened out)
- **File**: `dataset/public/Irwanto_2024_ID_SRL_Blended_Learning/Data for SPSS.xlsx`
- **Key Constructs Mapped**: Online Self-Regulated Learning Questionnaire (OSLQ) constructs on a 1–5 scale: Time Management (`TM1`), Environment Structuring (`ES1`), Task Strategies (`TS1`), Goal Setting (`GS1`), and Self-Evaluation (`SE1`).

---

## Public Study 3 (Student Perception About Online and Blended Learning)
- **Title**: Dataset Student Perception About Online Learning
- **Institution**: Universitas Negeri Makassar (UNM), Indonesia
- **Year**: 2021
- **Repository**: Mendeley Data
- **Mendeley Data DOI**: [10.17632/8myyf5t45z.3](https://doi.org/10.17632/8myyf5t45z.3)
- **Sample Size**: 250 raw / 229 validated undergraduate student respondents (21 straight-line/careless responses screened out)
- **File**: `dataset/public/Makassar_2021_Student_Perception_Online_Learning/Dataset Student Perception Online Learning.xlsx`
- **Key Constructs Mapped**: Teaching delivery effectiveness, task tracking & submission comfort, schedule adaptability, in-person vs. digital focus preference, and institutional modality continuation support.
